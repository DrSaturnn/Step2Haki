This curve stays rigid, and three consecutive vertebrae meet the stated wedging criterion. **Schmorl nodes support the diagnosis but are not required**; their absence would not overturn the other findings.

## Defining criteria

- **Scheuermann radiographic pattern** — anterior wedging of **at least 5° in at least three consecutive thoracic vertebrae**.
- **Correction test** — a postural curve corrects with prone positioning or active extension; a structural curve remains rigid.
