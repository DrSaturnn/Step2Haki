## Build notes for the fresh chat (lessons from the first five)
- s152build.py <brief> runs everything; then `verify.py <folder> --mech --fast`, and `grep -v ^ok` the output.
- shingle_check flags any 10-word run shared with a cited quote, including the live L lines: reword carried lines
  rather than copying them (run `shingle_check.py <folder>/brief.html <folder>/claim_map.json --bank <bank id> --show 30`).
- V1: an acronym must be written out at first use as "long name (ACR)", "ACR (long name)", or as initials of
  consecutive words; "CATCH-22 (" does not match, "CATCH (" does; a hover body counts.
- N1: a part with a digit that cites only L lines needs currency; s152lib.write adds "stable" automatically and
  lists them in stable_numbers.txt (review it); pass cur='flagged' for real thresholds carried from live.
- Ladder "when" cells with words or numbers must be t(...) parts, not plain strings.
- tail_start: briefs already on "Practice" with an id (kawasaki kd-bank, cyanotic-chd chd-bank) keep that header
  and need no header tail_fix; the others rename "Scenario bank" to "Practice" with a new id.
- disp.py phrases resolve against claim text (plain, lowercase); after rewording a line, re-check the phrase.
- Hubs (ped-murmur, newborn-cyanosis, myocarditis) need repair/s152/40_presentations.json (Presentations count
  +3, plain links; current count on the page is 25).
