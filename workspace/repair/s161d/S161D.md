## Cause

s146c zooms the page to 110% on windows 1500 px and wider; the desktop sidebar's height, calc(100vh - 32px) (s141), is
zoomed with it, so the panel ran past the window: bottom at 972 of 900 at 1600 x 900, 1170 of 1080 at 1920 x 1080. With
every group open and the list scrolled to its end, the last rows sat below the screen. Under 1500 px (no zoom) it fit.
