# Brief consolidation plan (draft for approval)

Principle (yours, 2026-10-03): titles name the broadest topic the content reasonably covers; briefs on very similar topics merge; a brief that truly stands alone stays. The decision clause that used to sit in a title moves to the subtitle line, which already carries the question.

Result if approved as drafted: 216 briefs become 168. 42 merges combine 90 briefs; 86 others are retitled in place; the rest keep their title.

How it would run:

1. **Retitles first, one batch.** Mechanical: the h4 changes, every Pairs with reference is rewritten to match. Brief ids, links and reader progress do not change. No content or question changes. Low drift risk.
2. **Merges after, one cluster per batch**, by the brief-migration procedure (claim map, every clue and question accounted for, independent review to 0 CRITICAL). Question ids and reader progress carry over; the absorbed brief ids become aliases so old links still resolve.

Rule 14 still applies: where a broader title promises content the brief does not have yet (marked *needs content*), the title waits until that content is written, or I use the narrower title.
