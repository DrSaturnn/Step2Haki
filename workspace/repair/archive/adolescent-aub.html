• Adolescent with heavy menses since menarche, easy bruising and a family history of bleeding → Von Willebrand panel (normal PT/aPTT doesn't rule it out)
  • Large fibroid with anemia before planned surgery → GnRH agonist short-term (osteoporosis risk beyond 6 months; use add-back)
  • 58 yo F with no period for 18 months, now spotting → Endometrial cancer until proven otherwise → transvaginal ultrasound or biopsy
