• 13 yo with 2 months of progressive unilateral knee pain, worse at night, with a firm tender mass over the distal femur → Order plain radiographs → Osteosarcoma
  • 5 yo with unilateral leg pain, temperature 39 C and refusal to bear weight → Obtain inflammatory markers with urgent imaging → Osteomyelitis or septic arthritis
  • 4 yo with bilateral night-time leg pain that is worse after active days, with a normal examination and no systemic symptoms → Growing pains → A clinical diagnosis
  • 6 yo with classic growing pains; which management is inappropriate → Knee braces during activity → Bracing and activity limitation are not recommended
