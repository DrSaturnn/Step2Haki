• Tea-colored urine + RBC casts + proteinuria + HTN, 2 weeks after sore throat → Post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis → low C3, positive ASO
  • Well child with isolated microscopic hematuria, normal blood pressure and no proteinuria → Urine calcium/creatinine ratio (hypercalciuria is the most common cause)
  • Sexually active adolescent with dysuria and sterile pyuria → Chlamydia/gonorrhea NAAT (also: renal TB, interstitial nephritis, partially treated UTI, appendicitis)
  • 6 yo boy with 1 day of bright red urine; no dysuria, urgency, frequency or abdominal pain; no trauma, recent infection or medications; BP 100/65, other vital signs normal; no edema and no flank or suprapubic tenderness; normal genital exam; urinalysis: blood 3+, protein negative, leukocyte esterase and nitrite negative, no bacteria, 2 to 4 WBC/hpf, more than 100 RBC/hpf, no casts → Renal and bladder ultrasonography → painless nephrolithiasis is the leading concern in a young child; the scan also excludes a renal mass and avoids radiation
  • 9 yo M with headache, lethargy and 2 days of low urine output, 2 weeks after a sore throat that resolved; BP 170/102, periorbital, scrotal and pedal edema, normal CBC, creatinine 1.9, urine with moderate blood and 2+ protein → Furosemide → poststreptococcal glomerulonephritis with volume overload
