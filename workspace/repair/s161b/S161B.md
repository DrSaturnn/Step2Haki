## Changes (all in the s143 sidebar component and its styles, one in the s141 nest script, one in the mode switches)

1. setBrief: the rail always sits under byId[id] (the system tile; Presentations-only briefs keep their Presentations
   tile). Only that tile gets aria-current; the other gets data-also (accent dot, weight 600).
2. A group the sidebar opened itself (g.auto) closes when the current brief lives in another group; the list is held
   still (scrollTop compensated on the tile in view). A group folded or unfolded by hand is never auto-closed.
3. Click on either tile goes to the brief; on the current brief's tile it brings that tile into view (pref and the
   s146c re-seat removed).
4. CSS for #axn a.axn-b[data-also].
5. s141 nest(): returns when window.AXN, so the brief's rail is no longer moved into the hidden old #navlinks on every
   brief change.
6. Browsing Presentations: a click on a Presentations tile sets `stay`; while set, keep() never scrolls toward the rail
   and follows the current brief's Presentations tile when it has one, holding still when it has none. A click on a
   system tile or a step clears it. The phone index reopens on that tile.
7. Shelf card under the search pill (replaces the display sheet's shelf row): accent chip with a shelf icon, "Shelf"
   label over the shelf name, chevron; the page's .shelfsel select lies invisibly over the card, so the system picker
   opens and the shelf scripts work unchanged. The brand subtitle keeps "Step 2 · Family Med · Peds".
8. Flat theme on unless localStorage ax-flat is '0' (a reader who turned flat off keeps sculpted).
