## Q1 Fetal alcohol syndrome (peds-cms-8e3e76)
- Owner teratogens teaches FAS from the facial triad, but only against fetal hydantoin syndrome and syphilis (clefting and digit hypoplasia decide). NBME set it against chromosomal syndromes (Down, trisomy 13, fragile X, Klinefelter) and congenital toxoplasmosis, with no maternal alcohol history at all. New look-alike (Down syndrome: upslanting, not short, fissures) = new angle.

- Conflict: none. The NBME explanation's "leading cause of intellectual disability" (it is a leading preventable, nonheritable cause) and "downturned" fissures in Down syndrome (they slant upward) were not transferred.
