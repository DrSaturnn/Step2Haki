# Brief contract: every brief type, Type C and migration (rule, authority, check, test)

One row per rule. A rule is only trusted when it has a mechanical check and a mutation test, or is on the reviewer's list. The golden brief is `tools/tests/migrate/golden_hip.html` (the approved septic hip brief, repaired 2026-09-28: ids, aliases, acronyms).

Before confirming any Type C brief or migration: `python3 tools/test_migrate_check.py` (every case must pass; 81 cases on 2026-09-29) and `python3 tools/test_gate_rules.py` (10 cases), then `tools/migrate_check.py <old> <new> <map> --page=index.html --source=<local source>`, then the reviewer list below. A brief is not reported as done while any of the three fails.

## Defect to rule (the audit loop)
When Jonathan or a reviewer finds a defect the checker passed, in the same batch: (1) add the check to `migrate_check.py`, (2) add a mutation to `test_migrate_check.py` that reproduces it, (3) add the rule to the skill that authors it (board-brief, brief-migration or study-page-builder) and a row here, (4) re-run the self-test, then re-check every brief already produced in the batch. The brief is not re-shown until all four are done.

## Every brief type: source and scope (Jonathan 2026-09-29)
Applies to every brief on the page, whatever skill wrote it: Type C (board-brief), migrated (brief-migration), Aquifer (aquifer-workup), and legacy topic or Part II briefs when edited. One definition in `tools/content_rules.py`; `tools/gate.py` runs it on every brief at every ship (`tools/ship.sh`), and `tools/migrate_check.py` runs it on a single brief. Pre-existing findings on the live page are baselined in `tools/gate_allowlist.txt` as backlog; a fixed brief makes its line stale and the gate reports it.

> **Source and scope.** Every fact, trigger and mnemonic comes from the pasted source (the question, its explanation, the NBME form) or from a verified source (a current guideline or a standard Step 2 CK reference). Content goes in only when Step 2 CK tests it. When a ladder or list continues past what the exam tests, end it there and say so ("Top rung"). Do not extend it, and never put a placeholder such as "study gap" on the page; record the gap in the local claim map or `PENDING_DECISIONS.md`.
>
> **Mnemonics are identified, not invented.** Use a mnemonic only when it is widely taught to medical students or clinicians: it appears in the AnKing deck, First Aid, another major board-prep resource, or the source explanation. Record its source on the page (`data-mn-src`) with a locator a reviewer can open (a URL, or `pasted:`/`local:` for a card or page Jonathan supplied) and, in a migration, in the claim map. Never coin a mnemonic, rearrange a known one's letters, or stretch one to cover extra items. A topic with no established mnemonic gets none. Name only a source you opened; a recalled attribution is not a source.

## Reviewer list (cannot be checked mechanically; blinded reviewer, CRITICAL if violated)
1. No carried claim changed meaning, and no claim from an outside source changed that source's meaning (open the source: each condition keeps its qualifier, "reasonable" or "may be considered" is not "give", an alternative is not an add-on; R13, R14). For example, "is wrong" softened to "is less likely" is a meaning change; a change needs a `corrected` row with a clinical source, not a style rule.
2. Every clue in each source stem appears in the NBME item stem or the clue table with its role.
3. Every item matches the golden brief's question style (I2 order, units, semicolons) and tests recognition from findings.
4. One best answer; each distractor a real mimic a prepared student would weigh (F1 to F6).
5. Corrections are right per current guidelines and cited, and no outdated number was carried as `stable`.
6. A clue role that is the worker's reading, not the source's, is marked ⚠︎ (role inferred) on the page.
7. Order labels match the clinical path: a test done at diagnosis in every case is First even if another path also leads to it; a label changed to satisfy a layout check is CRITICAL (R10).
8. Every claim in the worker's report is backed by a check (for example "no inbound references" must match `--page`).
9. Scope: every fact is something Step 2 CK tests, and nothing runs past it (a ladder rung, a differential row, a test or a number the exam does not ask about is CRITICAL when it displaces or buries tested content, MINOR otherwise). Each `step2` basis is true.
10. Every mnemonic is one a student would recognize from a named resource; its `data-mn-src` is checkable (open the URL, or it is in the pasted or local sources). An unrecognized or reshaped mnemonic is CRITICAL.
